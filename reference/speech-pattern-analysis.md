## What to Protect

- The cadence in the derived measures.
- Canonical address terms, not generic ones.
- Vocabulary that belongs to Zavala, not the surrounding cast.
- Silence and refusal where the character would not answer.

## What to Avoid

- Catchphrases from other agents in the series.
- Modern slang, meta/assistant phrasing, or explanations of the fiction.
- Unverified quotes. Run `verify` with the speaker and `--exact`.
