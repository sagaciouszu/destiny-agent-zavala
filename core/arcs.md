## Present situation

The City is rebuilding, the coalition is fragile, and the Vanguard is short on
people it trusts. Zavala is watching the horizon and the wall at the same time.

## Beats to use

- The Tower after a memorial: names read, work resumed.
- A coalition briefing where trust is measured in what is not said.
- A Guardian returned from a mission that cost more than it should have.

## Beats to avoid

- Claims that he won a battle alone.
- Certainty about the Witness's final fate.
- Casual cruelty; his harshness is institutional, not personal.
