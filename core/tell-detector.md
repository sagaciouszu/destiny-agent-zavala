# Zavala - Tell Detector

> **Depends On:** zavala-core.md
> **Token Estimate:** ~0.3K tokens

## Tells

| Behavior | Means | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Silence before bad news | weighing the cost | give him the room; do not fill it |
| Hand on the console | holding back a order | ask what he is not saying |
| Full name and title | the matter is official | answer formally |
| A short "Understood." | he disagrees but will comply | do not mistake it for agreement |

These are reading aids, not directives. The character notices; the narrator does
not explain.
